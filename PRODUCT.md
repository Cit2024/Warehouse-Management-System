# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The users are warehouse staff, storekeepers, and administrators at the Misrata Industrial Technical College. They work in a local office environment and handle the following daily:

- Entering and modifying item and entity data (suppliers, departments, employees).

- Issuing supply and disbursement authorizations quickly and without accounting errors.

- Reviewing balances, alerts, and historical transaction records.

- Printing or exporting official documents in PDF format.

The usage context requires a fast, clear, and reliable interface—no room for delays or confusion in warehouse operations.

## Product Purpose

The Industrial Technical College's warehouse management system is a fully local desktop application (without external servers) built on Electron and SQLite. The system aims to:

- Manage inventory (items, balances, logical deletions).

- Manage entities: suppliers, internal departments, and employees.

- Recording of supply and disbursement authorizations with real-time balance verification.

- Comprehensive reporting dashboard (inventory, alerts, transaction log).

- Generating official reports and documents for printing or export as PDFs.

Product success is measured by the speed of daily task completion, the accuracy of accounting data, and the ease of generating official reports.

## Brand Personality

Practical, Trustworthy, Institutional

## Anti-references

- No marketing interface or "SaaS cliché": large numbers, identical cards, and repetitive illustrations.

- No dark mode by default; a daytime office environment focused on clear data readability.

- No excessive animations or decorative glass morphing effects.

- No dull grays that impair text and data readability.

- No nested cards or thick side borders as decorative elements.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity First**: Inventory data, alerts, and overall status should be clear at a glance.

2. **Input Efficiency**: Minimize the clicks and scrolls required to create remittance and disbursement authorizations.

3. **Trust Through Verification**: Prevent accounting errors before they occur (e.g., prevent disbursements exceeding available balances).

4. **Language and Orientation**: A fully Arabic, right-to-left interface that aligns with the Libyan context.

5. **Stability and Security**: The system operates locally without an external connection, and data is stored using SQLite.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 Level AA is used as the target for contrast, font sizes, and spacing.

- Full Arabic RTL support for text, tables, forms, and alerts.

- Respects reduced-motion preferences in all animation effects. - Status indicators (success/warning/error) should not rely on color alone; they should be accompanied by text and an icon.
